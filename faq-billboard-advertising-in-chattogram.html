What billboard advertising is available in Chattogram and beyond?

Outside Dhaka and Sylhet we operate LED sites in Chattogram, Cox’s Bazar, Rajshahi, Cumilla, Rangpur, Bogura, Narayanganj and Feni — ten cities in total. Regional sites tend to be the most efficient reach in the network.

Two things regional buying gets you

Efficiency. A single face in Cumilla or Rangpur covers a much larger proportion of that city than any one Dhaka site covers of Dhaka. If you are measuring reach per taka, the regions usually win.

Distribution support. If you sell through a regional distributor network, a visible presence in their town does something for the trade relationship that a Dhaka billboard never will. Salespeople notice. So do retailers.

Cox’s Bazar swings hard with the tourist season. If that is your market, book around the season rather than the calendar quarter.
