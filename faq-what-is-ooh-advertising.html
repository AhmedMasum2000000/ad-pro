What is OOH advertising?

Out-of-home advertising is any advertising that reaches people while they are out of the house — billboards, LED screens, transit branding, airport panels, mall displays and street furniture. In Bangladesh it is dominated by roadside billboards, increasingly digital ones.

Call for price See the sites

Out-of-home — OOH, if you want the abbreviation everyone in the industry uses — is the oldest advertising medium there is, and one of the very few that has never been switched off, skipped, blocked or scrolled past.

That last point is not a sales line. It is structural. You cannot install an ad blocker on a junction.

Why it still works in a country this connected

Bangladesh has enormous mobile penetration and a genuinely crowded digital ad market. So why does a screen at a traffic signal still earn its keep?

  • Nobody is trying to avoid it. It arrives without asking for attention, which is a different psychological transaction from an interruption.
  • The dwell time is real. Dhaka traffic is famously slow. That is a problem for the city and an asset for a well-placed screen.
  • It is a public signal. Being on a major junction says something about a business that a targeted feed advert cannot say, because everyone sees it — including your competitors, your distributors and your future staff.
  • It lifts the things that follow it. Search, direct traffic and branded queries tend to move with a run. It rarely gets the credit, because there is no click to attribute.
