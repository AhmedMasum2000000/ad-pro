The screen

It is a landscape screen, which is the shape most brand lock-ups and film cuts are already built for. The panel is P4 — 4mm between pixels, which reads cleanly from about 4 metres back and further, so it is built for the traffic passing it rather than for someone standing underneath. Artwork is delivered at 1280p X1920p in MP4. Minimum booking is 60 min/day, and every campaign closes with a log summary showing when each spot actually played.

Who it reaches

Dhaka carries the densest and slowest traffic in the country, which is what makes an out-of-home second here worth more than a second anywhere else. At this size the rule is one idea and one lock-up — short copy, high contrast, and the brand mark large enough to read at a glance. Screen time is sold by the minute per day; tell us the window you want and we will confirm what is open on this face.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to advertise on Green Road Crossing, U-turn?

Screen time on this site is quoted rather than published, because the price depends on how many minutes a day you take and how long the campaign runs. Call +880 1316-743003 or email info@adprobd.com with your dates and you will have a written price, production included, within one working day.

What size is the Green Road Crossing, U-turn screen?

22ft × 12.5ft, running artwork at 1280p X1920p. Files are supplied as MP4, on a P4 panel — 4mm pixel pitch.

What hours does the Green Road Crossing, U-turn billboard run?

9am-11pm, which is 13 hours of on-air time a day. There is a maintenance break at 2:00pm - 3:00pm. Minimum booking is 60 min/day.

Which direction does Green Road Crossing, U-turn face?

It faces New Market, and reads clearly from roughly 4 metres back and beyond. Traffic approaching from that side has the screen in view for the length of the queue.
