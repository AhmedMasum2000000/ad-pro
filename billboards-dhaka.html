Booking in Dhaka

Screen time is sold by the minute per day, with a minimum of 60 min/day. Artwork is supplied as MP4 and can be changed the same day, so a campaign can carry a different message in the morning and the evening without a reprint. Every booking closes with a log summary showing when each spot played.

The largest face here is 50ft × 30ft at SKS Tower, Flyover View, Mohakhali. The smallest is 6.5inch × 9.5inch at Police Plaza Entry Gate (South side), which is the one to take when the message is short and the traffic is close.
