Narayanganj

Narayanganj is a 16ft × 10ft LED screen in Narayanganj, a compact face, close to the traffic rather than above it, facing Fotulla. The screen runs 10am-10pm, with a maintenance break at 1:00pm - 2:00pm.

The screen

It is a landscape screen, which is the shape most brand lock-ups and film cuts are already built for. Artwork is delivered at 1920p X 1080p in MP4. Minimum booking is 30 min/day, and every campaign closes with a log summary showing when each spot actually played.

Who it reaches

Narayanganj concentrates its traffic through a handful of junctions, so a single well-placed screen reaches a large share of the city rather than a slice of it. At this size the rule is one idea and one lock-up — short copy, high contrast, and the brand mark large enough to read at a glance. Screen time is sold by the minute per day; tell us the window you want and we will confirm what is open on this face.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to advertise on Narayanganj?

Screen time on this site is quoted rather than published, because the price depends on how many minutes a day you take and how long the campaign runs. Call +880 1763-924619 or email info@adprobd.com with your dates and you will have a written price, production included, within one working day.

What size is the Narayanganj screen?

16ft × 10ft, running artwork at 1920p X 1080p. Files are supplied as MP4.

What hours does the Narayanganj billboard run?

10am-10pm. There is a maintenance break at 1:00pm - 2:00pm. Minimum booking is 30 min/day.

Which direction does Narayanganj face?

It faces Fotulla. Traffic approaching from that side has the screen in view for the length of the queue.
