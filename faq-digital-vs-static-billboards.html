Digital or static billboards — which is better in Bangladesh?

Neither is better; they do different jobs. Static gives you sole occupancy of a face for a month or more, which suits always-on presence. Digital gives you a share of a loop you can change the same day, which suits launches, offers and anything with a date on it.

This is the question I get asked most, usually phrased as though one answer is obviously right. It is not. They are different instruments, and choosing badly is a more expensive mistake than choosing a slightly worse site.

What the table does not tell you

Two things worth knowing before you decide.

Sole occupancy is genuinely different. On a static face, your brand is that junction for a month. Nobody follows you eight seconds later. There is a psychological weight to that which a share of a loop does not quite reproduce, and for category-leader positioning it still matters.

But motion is hard to ignore. A screen that changes while somebody is sitting at a red light pulls the eye in a way a static panel cannot. If your message has a date, a price or an offer attached, digital does the job better and lets you correct it if the offer changes.

How I would actually choose

  • Message has a deadline, a price or a launch date → digital.
  • You want to own a junction for a season → static.
  • You need to be live this week → digital, comfortably.
  • Budget is thin and you want the longest possible presence → digital, short daily minutes over a longer run.
  • You are defending a category position against a competitor on the same road → static, and take the better face.
